About Smart College ERS (Education Record System)

The Smart College Education Record System provides an authoritative, fault-tolerant infrastructure designed to modernize collegiate academic record keeping, statutory compliance, continuous evaluation logging, and secure data centralization. Engineered to meet strict FERPA mandates and regional accreditation standards, the platform unifies real-time institutional metrics across student, faculty, and administrative tiers.
